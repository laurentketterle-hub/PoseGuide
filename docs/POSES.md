# POSES.md — Standing Pose Families Catalog

## Overview
This document catalogs standing pose families supported by PoseGuide, along with photo tips for best results.

## Pose Families

### 1. Standing Neutral
- **Description**: Feet shoulder-width apart, arms at sides
- **Difficulty**: Beginner
- **Use Case**: Baseline comparison, posture analysis
- **Photo Tips**: Stand against a plain wall, good lighting from front

### 2. Warrior Series
- **Warrior I**: Front knee bent 90°, back leg straight, arms overhead
- **Warrior II**: Wide stance, front foot forward, back foot 90°, arms parallel to floor
- **Warrior III**: Standing on one leg, torso and back leg parallel to floor
- **Photo Tips**: Side angle works best to show knee alignment

### 3. Tree Pose (Vrksasana)
- **Description**: Standing on one leg, other foot on inner thigh/calf
- **Difficulty**: Intermediate
- **Photo Tips**: Full-body front view, ensure both feet visible

### 4. Triangle (Trikonasana)
- **Description**: Wide stance, one hand to floor/ankle, other arm up
- **Difficulty**: Intermediate
- **Photo Tips**: Side profile preferred to show torso alignment

### 5. Mountain (Tadasana)
- **Description**: Feet together, arms at sides or overhead, spine elongated
- **Difficulty**: Beginner
- **Photo Tips**: Front view, feet together, natural lighting

### 6. Chair (Utkatasana)
- **Description**: Knees bent as if sitting, arms overhead
- **Difficulty**: Beginner
- **Photo Tips**: Side angle to show knee bend depth

### 7. Standing Forward Fold
- **Description**: Hinging at hips, torso folded forward
- **Difficulty**: Beginner
- **Photo Tips**: Side profile, ensure back is visible

### 8. Eagle (Garudasana)
- **Description**: Standing on one leg, arms and legs wrapped
- **Difficulty**: Advanced
- **Photo Tips**: Front or 45° angle, good contrast background

### 9. Dancer (Natarajasana)
- **Description**: Standing on one leg, back foot held behind
- **Difficulty**: Advanced
- **Photo Tips**: Side profile, capture full extension

### 10. Side Angle
- **Extended Side Angle**: Front knee bent, one hand on floor, other arm extended
- **Revolved Side Angle**: Twisted variation with opposite elbow to knee
- **Photo Tips**: Side angle, ensure torso rotation is visible

## General Photo Tips

1. **Lighting**: Natural, diffused light from front or side — avoid backlighting
2. **Background**: Plain, contrasting to body — avoid busy patterns
3. **Distance**: Full body in frame, ~2-3m distance from camera
4. **Clothing**: Form-fitting clothes for accurate joint detection
5. **Camera Height**: At mid-torso level, not angled up or down
6. **Multiple Angles**: Capture front, side, and 45° for best analysis
7. **Stability**: Use tripod or stable surface for consistency
8. **Resolution**: Minimum 1080p for accurate landmark detection
